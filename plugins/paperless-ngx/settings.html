Paperless-ngx

Browse your scanned documents from widgets and wallpapers. Credentials are stored encrypted; widgets never see them directly.

Examples: http://paperless.local:8000, http://192.168.1.50:8000, https://paperless.example.com. No trailing slash needed.

Generate in Paperless: tap your profile picture (top-right) → My Profile → scroll to API Auth Token. Encrypted at rest with the device Keystore.
