MQTT

Connect to your MQTT broker. Once configured, widgets can subscribe to topics and publish messages via iappyx.plugin('mqtt').*.

URL formats
  • tcp://192.168.1.10:1883 — plain MQTT, default port 1883. Credentials travel cleartext on your LAN.
  • ssl://broker.example.com:8883 — MQTT over TLS. Default port 8883.
  • ws://192.168.1.10:9001 — MQTT over WebSocket. Default port 9001 on most brokers (Mosquitto requires a separate listener block in mosquitto.conf).
  • wss://broker.example.com:443 — MQTT over secure WebSocket. Default port 443 or 8084.

Include the scheme and port. The plugin is restricted to trusted Wi-Fi by default, so a LAN IP is the typical case.

Most brokers require unique IDs. Leave blank to auto-generate.
